[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/). The Pexels license permits free use and does
not require attribution; see the [license terms](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
The original individual photo-page URLs were not retained when the files were
downloaded, so no photographer-specific attribution is available in this guide.
Do not treat stock subjects as the fictional designers named in the prototype
or imply their endorsement.
